# Contributing to Suflir

Contributions are welcome. Suflir is maintained by Anas
([anasabdur.com](https://anasabdur.com)), and every change goes through the
maintainer's review before it becomes part of the project.

## How to contribute

1. **Ask first for anything big.** Open an issue describing the feature or
   change and wait for the maintainer's go-ahead before building it. Small bug
   fixes and typo fixes can go straight to a pull request.
2. **Fork the repository** and make your change on a branch in your fork.
3. **Test it.** Run `./install.sh` and check that the app still builds and
   works.
4. **Open a pull request** against `main`, explaining what changed and why.
5. **Wait for review.** Only the maintainer merges. A pull request may be
   accepted, sent back with requested changes, or declined.

## Rules

- Nobody pushes directly to `main` except the maintainer.
- Keep the copyright notice, the license and the "Made by anasabdur.com"
  credit in place.
- By contributing, you agree that your contribution is released under the
  project's [MIT License](LICENSE).
