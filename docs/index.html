Suflir is a free teleprompter that sits under the MacBook notch, right next to the camera, and scrolls as you speak. No word limit.

Thanks for joining me today. I want to share three things we learned this quarter, and what they mean for next year.

Install

Pick either way. Both install the same app.

1 Download

Download Suflir.dmg
  1. Open Suflir.dmg and drag Suflir into Applications.
  2. Open Suflir. macOS blocks it the first time, because the app is not signed with a paid Apple developer account.
  3. In System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

2 Terminal

curl -fsSL https://suflir.anasabdur.com/get.sh | bash
  1. Open Terminal, paste the line above and press Return.
  2. Suflir is downloaded, put in Applications and opened, with no macOS warning.

Free and open source · Source on GitHub · Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.

What it does

Follows your voice

The text moves as you speak and waits when you pause. Words you have said fade out so you never lose your place.

Or a steady scroll

Prefer a fixed pace? Pick a speed and let it roll.

No word limit

Paste a five minute pitch or an hour long lecture. Keep as many scripts as you like in the library.

Hidden from screen sharing

The prompter can stay out of screen shares and recordings, so only you see it.

Out of the way

It lives in the menu bar, never steals focus, and can be made see-through.

Private

No account and no tracking. Your scripts stay on your Mac.
